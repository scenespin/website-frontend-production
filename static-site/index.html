Create, Produce, Direct

One connected workflow: build the story first, expand visual coverage, then carry scene and shot context through to delivery.

Create

Screenplay workspace

  • Fountain-native screenplay editing
  • Context-aware AI agents for rewrites and scene work
  • Character, location, and prop extraction from script context

Produce

Coverage packages

  • Generate or upload image assets
  • Character pose, location angle, and prop angle coverage
  • Reusable visual assets tied to screenplay entities

Direct

Shot-linked execution

  • Generate or upload first frames by scene and shot
  • Review sequence flow in Scene Builder and Shot Board
  • Launch video workflows with continuity-aware metadata

Product Thesis

Write first, then carry story context all the way through production.

Wryda was designed around a screenplay-first workflow. The writing environment remains the source of truth, while downstream planning, asset generation, and handoff stay tied to the same scene and shot context.
