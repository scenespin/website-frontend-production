Technology

Provider-agnostic workflow architecture built around screenplay context.

Wryda is positioned around continuity and orchestration rather than a single model vendor. The value is in how story context moves through the workflow.

Context-first foundation

Story information begins in the screenplay and remains linked to characters, locations, props, scenes, and shots.

Reusable visual coverage

Character, location, and prop references are organized for reuse so downstream generation and planning stay consistent.

Flexible execution

Approved assets and first frames can be handed into different generation workflows as needs change.

What the technology page is meant to communicate

The public site is no longer trying to be a self-serve product entry point. It is meant to show the product thesis, workflow architecture, and technical thinking behind the platform.

Workflow strengths

  • Screenplay-native authoring and AI assistance
  • Entity extraction and structured story data
  • Scene Builder and Shot Board planning model
  • Handoff from approved frames into production workflows

Presentation strengths

  • Easy to review for investors, employers, or collaborators
  • Live demo path remains available when needed
  • Public site can stay online very cheaply
  • No reliance on Vercel for the dormant public website
